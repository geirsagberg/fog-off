# Fog Off privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026.

Fog Off is an Android app that remembers where you have been and clears fog from those places on a map. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Your location stays on your device

Fog Off records your location to build your map. It stores location fixes, trips, routes you draw and the map areas you have visited in a database on your device.

- Fog Off has no account and no server. The developer never receives your location or any other data from the app.
- Fog Off has no ads and no analytics.
- Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer leave out the app's data.

## When Fog Off uses your location

- **Track trip:** while you track a trip, Fog Off uses your location and shows a notification.
- **Automatic tracking:** if you turn it on, Fog Off collects your location in the background, even when the app is closed, to clear fog where you go. It stays on until you turn it off in the app or remove the location permission.

Fog Off also uses Android's physical activity recognition to notice when you start or stop moving, so it can save battery. The detected activity, such as walking or cycling, is stored with your location fixes on your device.

## Services from Google

Fog Off uses Google services to work:

- **Google Maps** draws the map. To load the map, your device sends requests to Google, which can include your IP address, the map area you are viewing, device details and crash reports. Fog Off does not send your location history to Google Maps.
- **Google Play services** gives the app its location and activity updates.

Google handles this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Backups you make

You can back up your database to a file. Fog Off saves it only where you choose, such as a folder or a cloud drive. After that, the file is yours to look after.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall Fog Off, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete everything the app has stored. You can remove drawn routes in the app.

## Children

Fog Off is not meant for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
